TradeScanner Operating Guide

Step-by-step instructions for daily routines and setup trading
Step 1

Pre-Market Preparation (8:00 AM – 9:25 AM ET)

Run the Daily Setups Scan before the market opens to identify high-probability candidates:

  • 20-Day Breakout: The latest close broke higher than the highest close of the past 20 trading sessions.
  • Bull Flag: Surged >10% over 10 sessions, followed by tight consolidation within a 5% range over the last 5 days.
  • Reclaim MA20: Previously closed below its 20-day simple moving average and just pushed back above it.
  • Oversold & Bounce: Wilder 14-period RSI dipped under 30 (extreme fear) and closed higher today.
Step 2

Opening Range Establishment (9:30 AM – 9:45 AM ET)

Wait for the opening 15 minutes of regular market hours. During this period, institutions, algorithms, and market makers establish the session's initial high and low.

Rule of Thumb: Do NOT chase blind market orders during the first 5 minutes of regular hours. Let the range print first.
Step 3

Execute the ORB Scan (After 9:50 AM ET)

Switch the scan mode to Intraday ORB and click Execute Live Scan:

  • The scanner checks 5-minute bars across your selected stocks.
  • A valid breakout occurs only when a completed 5-minute bar CLOSES strictly above the highest high of the 15-minute opening range.
  • Notice the timestamp (e.g. 09:55, 10:05) indicating exact trigger time.
Step 4

Risk Management & Execution Protocol

A scan is a filter, not an automated buy order. Execute with disciplined structure:

Entry First bar closing above opening range high or retest of range high.
Stop Loss Midpoint of opening range, or lowest point of the breakout candle.
Target 2:1 to 3:1 Reward-to-Risk ratio or previous daily resistance.
Step 5

Command-Line Execution (Optional)

If you prefer running scans from your terminal, use:

python scan.py universe                      # Updates 5,943 symbols
python scan.py daily --symbols AAPL,NVDA,TSLA # Fast daily scan
python scan.py orb --at 10:15                 # Cutoff at 10:15 AM ET
